## 🔬 Deep Dive: Retinal OCT Analyser & Volumetric CNN Suite

> **NYUAD Center for Brain and Health · Senior Capstone Project in Medical Computer Vision**

An end-to-end clinical workstation and deep learning pipeline for high-resolution peripapillary Retinal Nerve Fiber Layer (RNFL) volumetric segmentation and multi-disease classification across Optovue Solix and Heidelberg OCT/OCTA devices.

- **Dense Anisotropic 3D U-Net (`AnisotropicRNFLUNet3D` ~20.9M params):** Built custom asymmetric axial downsampling strides `(1, 2, 1)` and en-face isotropic kernels `(3, 3, 3)` tailored to extreme OCT voxel anisotropy ($18.81\,\mu\text{m} \times 3.12\,\mu\text{m} \times 18.75\,\mu\text{m}$); evaluated via GPU-accelerated sliding-window inference with Gaussian importance weighting on the NYUAD Jubail Supercomputer.
- **Bi-Planar 2.5D Orthogonal Fusion (`VolumetricRNFLNet` ~6.58M params):** Developed an orthogonal multi-slice context network combining horizontal B-scans and vertical reslices with boundary regression and optical edge alignment. Achieved **$5.13\,\mu\text{m}$ MABE** on expert-corrected eyes, surpassing 3D on 34/40 volumes and 10/10 human-audited cases (**0.9407** cup IoU).
- **Hierarchical & Multi-Head Classification:** Implemented multi-head EfficientNet & ConvNeXt-V2 models for multi-label pathology classification (CNV, DME, Drusen, Normal) and 3-stage hierarchical decision trees (macular, vascular, diabetic, structural, fluid).
- **Physics-Informed Preprocessing:** Coupled multi-surface graph optimization with $C^\infty$ cubic B-Spline curvature regularization to eliminate high-frequency staircase boundary artifacts in deep CNN stems.
- **Clinical Workstation & Automated Auditing:** Built a FastAPI + Next.js interface with dynamic SVG boundary visualization and automated cohort report generation compiling publication-grade PDF clinical audits.
